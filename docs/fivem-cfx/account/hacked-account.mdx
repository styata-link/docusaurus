# Hacked

## I've been hacked! What do I do?

There is nothing much you can do, besides contact the [Cfx.re Support](https://support.cfx.re/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=13998409033628) team. 

When *(and if)* you get your account back, please do the following:

1. Setup 2FA
2. Reset your passwords to all accounts.
3. Do **NOT** share your account information with anyone, including putting it on a shared VPS.

:::tip[NOTE]
Cfx.re support can take weeks, if not months to get to your request. Be patient.
:::
