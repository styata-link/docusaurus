# Adheasive Crash

## I cant load up FiveM, cause I get this!

![](/img/adheasive.png)

## What causes this crash?

Crashes with the "adheasive.dll+000000" could potentially mean that: 

- Your BIOS is out of date, and you may need to update your BIOS <small>This can be caused by your 13/14th gen intel</small>.
- You have cheating software on your computer, even if its for other games

You can typically fix these crashes by updating your BIOS, and doing a Deep Scan with [Malwarebtyes](https://malwarebytes.com/) - and removing all files/malware that it has detected.


:::warning
Do not attempt to delete `adheasive.dll` from your FiveM files. It will cause the game to not let you in any servers protected by adheasive.
:::

## I am still crashing!

In most cases, your Windows installation could be the problem and typically the only way to resolve this issue is by reinstalling windows completely.

> How do I reinstall Windows?

- Go to "Reset this PC"
- Select "Remove Everything"
- Then Select "Cloud Download"

:::info[REMINDER]
This **WILL** delete **ALL** files on your system. You are more than welcome to take backups of files that you need.
:::
