# Early Exit Trap

## My game crashes with this crash!

![](/img/eet.png)

## What causes this crash?

- Your BIOS is out of date, and you may need to update your BIOS <small>This can be caused by your 13/14th gen intel</small>.
- Cheating software that is on your computer
- Mods that potentially can contain malware.
- Uninstalling software that might interfere with the game.

## How do I fix this?

- Upgrading your BIOS <small>This can be caused by your 13/14th gen intel</small>.
- Removing any cheating software from your system.
- Remove any mods that you have added to both FiveM/Grand Theft Auto V installation folders.
- Performing a Deep Scan with [Malwarebtyes](https://malwarebytes.com)
