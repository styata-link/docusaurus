## How to fix this crash

You can potentially fix this crash, by doing these steps below:

1. Disable your antivirus
    1. Don't worry, this may be temporary.
2. Allowlisting/Excluding all of Rockstar's folders, including FiveM.
3. Re-enabling your Antivirus.

:::note
If you still have these crashes, try disabling your antivirus - and reload FiveM.
:::
