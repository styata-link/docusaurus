# Missing Files

![](/img/missing-game-files.png)

## What causes this crash?

If you have modified your game location, such as moving your GTA5.exe to some other folder, and selecting it where you moved it to when opening FiveM, it can cause this crash.
I would recommend that you do not move your files. Please move them back, or fix your IVPath by following this [guide](https://forum.cfx.re/t/tutorial-correct-gta-v-installation-location-s-ivpath-fix/5258845).
