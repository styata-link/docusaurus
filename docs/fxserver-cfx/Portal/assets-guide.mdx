# Asset Guides

## How do I transfer assets to other accounts?

Transferring assets is easy, just follow these steps:

1. Go to your [Assets Portal](https://portal.cfx.re/assets/granted-assets)
2. Click on the little box 

![](/img/assett.png)

3. Click the "Transfer" button that is above those boxes (on the right side).
4. Type in their name, and click "transfer".

:::tip
If it says "invalid username" or something similar, its likely that you put the wrong username - or the person you're transferring the asset to already has it.
:::

> **WARNING** There are scams going around, requesting you to transfer an asset so they can "work" on it. Do not do this, as they will not return the asset.



## How do I upload assets?

You can upload your own assets [here](https://portal.cfx.re/assets/created-assets). Make sure its a `.zip` file, and not anything else - as stated in the next section.
