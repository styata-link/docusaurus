# Flashing your BIOS

Typically, we will ask you to update your BIOS if we feel like that can solve some of your problems. 
These problems are mostly found in EET errors or other scarce issues.

You can get your BIOS update by searching up your motherboard manufacturer on google. You can get that by going to: 
`WIN + R` and searching `MSINFO32`. It will be under "BaseBoard" <small>See image below.</small>


![](/img/RD4alc.png)


Search up your motherboard, with bios updates - and you should be able to find the most recent one.
AFter that, follow the guide below.

## Guide to Flash your BIOS

:::warning
We are not responsible for anything that happens to your computer. 
:::

1. Download BIOS
2. Put the two files on the USB stick
3. Run BIOSRenamer.exe
4. Restart Your PC
5. While it's starting spam the `F11`, `Delete`, `F2` (dependent on your motherboard) keys on your Keyboard
6. This will open BIOS. Press F7 to go to Advanced Mode
7. Go to the Tool menu to select ASUS EZ Flash 3 Utility and press Enter.
8. Press the Left arrow key to switch to the Drive field.
9. Press the Up/Down arrow keys to find the USB flash disk that contains the latest
10. BIOS, and then press `ENTER`.
11. Press the Right arrow key to switch to the Folder field.
12. Press the Up/Down arrow keys to find the BIOS file, and then press `ENTER` to
13. perform the BIOS update process.
